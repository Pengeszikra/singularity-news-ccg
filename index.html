Singularity New CCG

Rules

Pairing

Click one of your cards (near side), then one of the opponent’s cards (far side).

  • Positive + positive of opposite parity: sum
  • Negative + negative of the same parity: sum of absolute values
  • Zero + any non-zero: absolute sum × 3
  • Inverse values (e.g. 7 and −7): value × 4

Round

Keep pairing until nothing fits, then draw up to 4 cards. The opponent draws 2. When it holds 4 cards it takes revenge: it pairs against your hand and scores the absolute sum of what is left. When its deck is empty, the higher number wins.

City map

The game starts on the city map. Win a round to unlock the next district. Early districts use small decks, later ones bigger. The order is Q Q 4 Q 4 Q 4.

42

42 is the answer to everything. If anyone’s score lands on exactly $42M, they win the game instantly.

Money chips

Your money is shown as poker chips on the table: white = $1M, red = $5M, green = $25M, black plaque = $100M, purple plaque = $500M. They fly in when points are scored.

Auto

The Auto button lets the machine play your moves in both modes. Switch it off any time to take over.

4 players mode

  • You and 3 bots, 4 cards each. The starter plays exactly one card to the center.
  • Going clockwise, each player must play a card that pairs with the card on top, and may then keep going: further cards are allowed as long as each pairs with the card just placed. Every pair adds its score to the pot.
  • The first player who cannot play ends the chain: the one who played last takes the pot and leads next. Hands refill to 4.
  • After the singularity you have 7 seconds, otherwise you pass. Everyone’s revenue is halved at the singularity.

Who is who

  • Before the singularity the opponent is a Big Tech CEO, after it an AGI
  • Your points are $M revenue, the opponent’s are $M of burned resources
  • If a pair contains an unemployed card, the turn passes to the opponent with 5% chance (10% if both are unemployed)
  • The glowing frame shows whose half of the table is on turn

Singularity meter (0–12)

  • +1 every round
  • +1 for each Robot or Tech card in a pair
  • −1 for each Conspiracy card in a pair

News and layoffs

At 3, 6 and 9 the news turns scarier and a share of human cards becomes unemployed (value −2). Robots are safe.

The Singularity

  • Robots become 0 (wildcards)
  • Humans become Echoes: sign flipped
  • All scores ×2
  • Your revenue is halved, the AGI’s burned resources are doubled
  • You get only 7 seconds to choose a pair, otherwise the opponent moves instead
